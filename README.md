# FINTRAC Fines Calculator: AML Penalty Exposure (2026 Rules)

**Live demo:** https://zov911.github.io/fintrac-fines-calculator/

What could weak AML compliance cost your business? Since March 26, 2026, FINTRAC's administrative monetary penalties can be **up to 40× higher** than before. This calculator shows a business's maximum exposure in seconds.

## Features

- **Compliance program gaps:** compliance officer, policies, risk assessment, training and the two-year effectiveness review, each classified by violation tier
- **Missed reports:** STR, LCTR, LVCTR and EFTR, with per-report counts
- **Old vs new framework comparison**, with an animated "× higher" bar
- **Revenue-based cap** for businesses under the new rules: $20M, or 3% of gross global revenue above $20M
- **Business-impact estimate:** revenue at risk if a public penalty notice leads to banking de-risking
- Business vs individual limits, a sticky mobile total bar, automatic light/dark mode

## Why it exists

Regulatory change creates urgency. An interactive "what's your exposure?" tool turns a dry penalty table into a personal, shareable number, and it's a proven lead magnet for compliance consultancies and RegTech vendors.

## Tech

A single `index.html`: vanilla JS with no dependencies.
