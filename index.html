    How this works

    This tool shows the legal maximum. FINTRAC decides the real amount case by case. It looks at how much harm the violation caused, your compliance history, and, under the new rules, your ability to pay. When a business completely fails an obligation, FINTRAC has said it will generally apply the maximum available penalty. Serious cases are also published on FINTRAC's website.

    • Maximum per violation, old rules: minor $1,000; serious $100,000; very serious $500,000 for businesses ($100,000 for individuals).
    • Maximum per violation, new rules: minor $40,000; serious $4,000,000; very serious $20,000,000 for businesses (about $4,000,000 for individuals).
    • For businesses under the new rules, the total for multiple violations is capped at $20 million, or 3% of gross global revenue if revenue is above $20 million.
    • Tiers follow classifications used in published FINTRAC penalty notices. The new framework may move some compliance program violations to "very serious."

    This is an educational estimate, not legal advice. Sources: FINTRAC penalty framework changes, FINTRAC modernization updates.
